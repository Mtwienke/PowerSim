Target: Gao, Yu, Kamel and Yang (2025), IEEE TNNLS 36(5), 8049–8059, [DOI 10.1109/TNNLS.2024.3437741](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNNLS.2024.3437741). Evidence is drawn from the repository's audited full text (`docs/paper-protocol.md`), pinned public examples (`docs/gao-source-audit.md`), source code and frozen artifacts. The public examples conflict with the journal and do not include the complete author data generator.

## Method correspondence

- **M0 analogue**: PowerSim uses pandapower/PYPOWER `rundcopp` (numerical interior-point DC OPF, no explicit solver-tolerance override, `numba=False`) plus finite-result and constraint validation. A validated solve is the reference, not an independently certified global optimum. Solver failure does not prove infeasibility. IEEE-14 and AC solver paths exist, but there are no corresponding report-scale learned results. Germany is DC-only.
- **M1 analogue**: L0-only supervised regression. **M2/M4/M5 analogues** in the pilot/reconstruction explicitly implement L0+L1, L0–L4 and L0–L5 respectively, but numerical definitions vary; these are not author implementations. M1-continued controls for additional epochs. Historical `baseline`, `physics-guided` and `without-L*` keep descriptive names, not published method labels; `without-L5` has the M4 loss set but a different training protocol.
- **M3 / DeepOPF is unavailable**. `M3-example` implements the visible Gao example's supervised objective plus normalized branch-penalty comparator, not external DeepOPF.

## Features, labels, losses and protocol differences

- Inputs: one real-valued **net active load in MW** per ordered in-service bus (`gross − fixed wind/PV`); negative inputs are legal. The load writer zeros original static injections and applies the net vector once. Outputs: conventional-source active dispatch in MW, ascending external grids then generators, including slack. No voltage/reactive generation is inferred; DC is not an AC OPF comparison. Net shunt MW follows the solver DC bus convention.
- Active source constraints are reference outputs within 0.001 MW of either bound; active branches are within 0.001 MW of either sign of configured `RATE_A` (lines and transformers); these masks differ from violation indicators. KKT sensitivities are local and exclude ill-conditioned/weakly active sets, rather than treating unavailable sensitivities as zero.
- Generic L0 is physical generation MSE; L1 masked sensitivity MSE; L2 penalizes the deviation of summed source derivatives from one; L3 penalizes reference-active generator derivatives; L4 penalizes reference-active branch derivative mismatches; L5 applies the balance-derivative penalty at random unlabeled perturbed inputs. Historical variants use mean-squared reductions, active-entry mask normalization, training-only z-scores and Gaussian perturbations scaled by 0.1 times per-bus training SD. All three reconstruction profiles use the `sum(J) != 0 && signed_max(J) < 10` availability mask, full selected-tensor reduction where applicable, and Gaussian SD 100 MW per coordinate for L5. `source-informed` uses MAE (except squared L1) and all applicable active sets; `journal-equations` uses squared reductions; `public-example` omits L3 and limits L4 to source example branch row 20. Equal loss weights are not invariant to scaling. Reconstruction uses a fixed 100 MW per-unit **model** scale, not the power network's base MVA; saved dispatch and inputs are already MW.
- Historical training: tanh, 3×200, Adam 0.001, batch 64, independent 100 epochs and best-validation selection. Pilot: tanh, same width/batch, Adam 0.01, 1,000 shared M1 plus 200 warm-started epochs, final and best retained. Reconstruction: 3×200 ReLU, batch 50, 1,000+200, Glorot weights, Keras-v2-style Adam in PyTorch, epsilon-hat 1e−7 and decay 2e−6, final primary checkpoint. Enabled weights are one; base rates are 0.001 for source-informed/public-example versus 0.01 for the journal-equations control. Author seed, exact sampling and final training details are not established. The two historical IEEE seeds also change site placement, training data and test inputs; they quantify joint protocol variability, not isolated initialization uncertainty.
- Scenario generation uses independent per-bus truncated relative Gaussian loads (typically 20% SD), seeded PQ placement for two wind and two PV sites, Weibull wind and beta irradiance with assumed conversion/dependence. Historical nameplate rate is `R / nominal gross load`; reconstruction uses the hypothesis `R / (335 MW + R)` with 185/230/300 MW. Realized share is sampled renewable MW divided by gross MW. Historical 0.9 thermal scale and attempted-case filtering differ from reconstruction 1.0 scale and collection to a successful-label quota. Equal printed rates do not imply equal load/weather or congestion distributions. No saved report-scale demand-only shift was identified.
- Decoder reconstructs all ordered net-load features from a frozen predictor. Historical single-layer tanh decoder (100 epochs) takes penultimate hidden features, uses normalized input MSE and validation q95. The pilot uses a two-layer 128-unit tanh decoder, 1,000 epochs, penultimate features and physical MAE. Reconstruction uses physical RMSE with fixed 6 MW and validation-based gates; all fit for 1,000 epochs with 200-unit ReLU layers, but source-informed/public-example use last-hidden features with two/three decoder layers and journal-equations uses penultimate features with two decoder layers. Gao Fig. 10 gives 7 MW and Table V gives 6 MW; the article's norm is not recovered. Old gates are validation-calibrated, not fitted on test labels. Rejection invokes a fresh numerical OPF; there is no projection or online adaptation.
- Gao Eq. (26) is strict `<1 MW`; Table V supports but does not prove generator-wise aggregation. Historical inclusive joint success is not directly comparable. Report strict element, max-row and mean-row metrics distinctly. The saved 1 MW L-infinity self-FGSM uses per-bus MW (not total load disturbance) and model-dependent perturbations; unchanged-target and fresh-OPF-target statistics answer different questions. The paper's exact attack target convention is unresolved.

## Germany: model provenance and scope

Xiong, Fioriti, Neumann, Riepin and Brown, *Prebuilt Electricity Network for PyPSA-Eur based on OpenStreetMap Data*, v0.7 ([Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18619025), [methodology DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04550-7)), ODbL 1.0; © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). See `powersim/benchmarks/germany30.LICENSE.md` for the bundled derivation's notice.

Historical v1 artifacts must use their serialized `network.json`, not the revised bundle:

| Network | Regional construction | Demand and generation | Branches and costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | Farthest-first graph seeds and shortest-path assignment of the 780-bus connected component | 30 demand nodes, 48 GW gross by source-bus count, 18 conventional sources including domestic slack (1.85× gross capacity); six fixed wind and six fixed PV injections in the nominal snapshot | 53 corridors; deterministic synthetic quadratic costs (slack linear coefficient 42 EUR/MWh); each coefficient is in the frozen manifest |
| v2 | Capacity-constrained Ward partition of the same component, 16–36 source buses per region | 30 demand nodes, 70 GW gross and 87.5 GW conventional capacity at 18 sources including slack; latitude-weighted wind/PV | 54 corridors; synthetic 38–72 EUR/MWh linear merit order plus quadratic terms; slack 85 EUR/MWh linear |

Both networks have a 100 MVA base and 380 kV equivalent corridors. Domestic AC lines and transformers are aggregated; cross-border exchange and HVDC are omitted. Nominal wind/PV (4.2/2.4 GW) is replaced, not added, by the same type of stochastic fixed injections used for scenario generation. Ratings/reactances are estimated, referred to 380 kV, combined as parallel reactance equivalents and rated to respect modeled member limits under that split. Transformer impedance is assumed 12%. There is no plant telemetry. Neither v1 nor v2 yields certified German-grid feasibility. The v2 reference validation uses 200 attempts per regime at 0.9 rating scale and does not generate learned results.

## Evidence that requires another computation

Saved proposals and references suffice for error matrices, validated DC flow/limit/cost calculations, failure coverage, observed gate decisions, existing fallback outcomes, and both saved FGSM target conventions. Additional **validation-only** decoder inference can supply a threshold grid for old artifacts where only q95 was saved. Controlled timing needs fresh inference and solver calls on identical inputs. A fresh demand-only test would need a declared new sampler and numerical labels (not retraining); matched current-v2 German learned results would need compatible checkpoints and new labels/training, and cannot be recovered by relabeling v1. Matched seed-only uncertainty and full DeepOPF need new experiments. No training or solver modification is authorized by the report's artifact-only mode.
